# Code of conduct

Siftora is built by and for people from many backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project, is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people whose first language is not English.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people. Explain the why behind a review comment.
- Assume good intent, and accept that the maintainer has the final say on what goes into the project.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language, of any kind and towards anyone.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a conversation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam and self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people from the project when this code is not followed, starting with a warning except in serious cases.

To report a problem privately, use the contact option on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or report the comment to GitHub with its **Report content** option. Reports are handled confidentially.
